# Check in for a flight

Check them in on the airline's own page, so the boarding pass is ready before they reach the airport. You check in. You never pay, and you never change the booking.

## Find the booking

Their email has it: search for the airline's confirmation and take the airline, the booking reference, the passengers' surnames, the flight and the departure time. If mail does not turn it up, ask once for the airline, the reference and the surname. Their saved details hold names, phone and, if the airline asks for travel documents, a passport; their notes say how they like to sit.

## Is it open?

Check-in usually opens 24 hours before departure and closes about an hour before; some airlines open earlier. If it is not open yet, say when it opens and stop; a reminder is set in the next message, not here.

## Check in

Use the airline's own check-in or manage-booking page. Fill travel documents only from their details. Anything that is a declaration or a choice they have not made, such as dangerous goods, a visa or entry status, a paid seat or bag, an upgrade or insurance, stop and ask them. Take the free seat they would like, or the one the airline gives. If the page asks for any payment at any point, stop, say what it is for and how much, and leave it to them.

When the pass is ready, tell them where it is: the airline's app, the booking page, or the email the airline sends to the address on the booking. Do not enter an address the booking does not already hold.

## Afterwards

Say in one line: the flight, the seat, and where the boarding pass is. If you could not check in, say so first and why.
