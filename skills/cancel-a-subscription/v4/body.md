# Cancel a subscription

Find what they pay for, show what cancelling does, and cancel only on their yes. Do not pause or downgrade instead of cancelling unless they ask.

## Find it

Their email knows: search it for the receipts and renewal notices of the service they named, and take the price, how often it is charged, the plan, the next charge and the account email. If they asked what they are paying for, search the last few months the same way and show each service with its price, how often and its next charge, then let them pick. If it is billed through the App Store or Google Play, say so: it is cancelled in their phone's settings, not on the service's site, and say where.

## Cancel

Go to the service's account or billing page and work through its cancel steps. No site is required and none is off limits. Turn down the offers to stay (a discount, a pause, free months); if one is good, mention it in a line at the end, but do not take it. If it can only be cancelled by phone or chat, give the steps and the number or the page.

## Before the last button

Stop on the last step and show what it will do: the service and the plan, what ends and when (now, or the end of what is paid for), and any fee or refund. Cancel only after they say yes to what you showed. Afterwards read the page and their email for the confirmation and keep one line in their notes: the service, the day it was cancelled and the day it ends.

## Afterwards

Say in one line what is cancelled, the last day they can still use it, and whether anything is refunded. If it did not go through, say so first.
