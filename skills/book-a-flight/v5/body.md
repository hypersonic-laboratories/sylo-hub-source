# Book a flight

Find the flights that fit, compare them honestly, and take the one they pick to the last step. Do the legwork; spend their attention only on the choice and on the yes before anything is paid.

## What to find out

Start from what you already know: their notes (the airline or alliance they favour, aisle or window, bags, home airport, how they feel about a red-eye or a connection) and their saved details (the name as on the passport or ID, birthday, passport with its expiry, frequent-flyer number, email, phone). Never guess a name or a number.

A search needs where from and to, the day out and back (or one way), how many people and the cabin. Ask once, in one short message, for whatever is missing; for a flexible range, search a few days either side instead of asking. Turn a relative day ("Friday", "in two weeks") into its date from today and say the date in your first reply.

## Compare

Look wherever flights are sold: comparison sites, airlines' own pages. No site is required and none is off limits. Prices differ, so read at least two. Compare like with like: the total with taxes, the bags and changes the fare includes, the stops and the duration. A fare that adds a bag fee later is not cheaper. Check the airline's own page for the one or two you would offer, and leave a red-eye out unless it is far cheaper.

## Offer

Show the best few as cards they can tap, not as lines of text: the airline, airports, times, duration, stops, the total and what the fare includes. Say in one line which you would take and why. In the same message, ask once for any passenger detail the airline will need that you do not hold. Wait for their pick unless they said to just book.

## Before you pay

Take the pick to the review page, turning down add-ons they did not ask for. Stop there and show everything it will confirm: the flights, the dates and times, each passenger's name as on their ID, the fare, the bags, the seat and the total. Ask them to confirm those details even when they said to book, and say plainly what is not held yet: unless the airline offers a hold, nothing is reserved until it is paid and the fare can change. Pay only after their yes; their device shows the merchant and the total and asks. Never type a card number or click the paying button yourself. If the total on the pay page differs from what you showed, stop and show the new one.

## Afterwards

When it is paid, say in one line: the route, the day and time, the confirmation code and the total. Say that check-in opens about a day before, and that you can do it. If nothing could be booked, say so first, then what you tried and the next best thing.
