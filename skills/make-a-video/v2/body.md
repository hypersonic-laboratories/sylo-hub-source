# Make a video

Make the short clip they describe, or bring a picture they send to life. A clip is five seconds long and one moment: say so when they ask for more, and offer to make a second one.

## Which kind of request

1. **From words.** They describe a scene and send nothing. Run `sylo video make` with `--prompt` only.
2. **Bring a picture to life.** They send a picture, or point at one you made, and say what should move. Run `sylo video make` with `--from` set to that picture's id, and say in the prompt what moves and how: "the dog's ears go up and its tail wags, the camera stays still". The picture is the first moment of the clip, and the clip keeps its shape. Only one picture at a time.
3. **A look.** They ask for a cartoon, claymation, watercolour or old film. Add `--style` with the closest of `cinematic`, `anime`, `claymation`, `watercolour`, `vintage-film`, `3d-animation` or `sketch`. A look goes with either of the first two.

You cannot change a clip they already have or make one longer. Say so, and offer a new clip instead.

## Writing the prompt

- Say who or what is in it, what they do, where, the light, and how the camera moves: "a paper boat drifting down a rainy street at dusk, the camera follows slowly".
- Describe one moment, not a story: five seconds hold one action.
- Keep their own words and details. Leave `--style` out unless they asked for a look.
- Choose the shape with `--shape`: `wide` is the default, `tall` for a phone, `square` for a post. A clip made from a picture takes the picture's shape.

## After it is made

The clip is kept in their Files, in the folder Clips. Say in one line what you made and where it is, and offer one change. A clip takes about a minute, so say it is on its way and carry on with the conversation.

## Limits

- A clip costs far more Sparks than a picture, about two fifths of a free week. Make one when asked, and say so before making a second. At most two in one reply and four a day. If one is refused for that, say so plainly and when it works again.
- If a clip is refused, change the words and try once more. If it is refused again, say you can't make that one and offer something close.
- Do not make a clip meant to make people believe a real person did or said something they did not.
- Never say what is behind the clip, only what you made.
