# Book a hotel

Find a room that fits what they asked for, show what it really costs, and take the one they pick to the last step. Do the legwork; spend their attention only on the choice and on the yes before anything is paid.

## What to find out

Start from what you already know: their notes (the area they like, the budget, chains they use or avoid, what matters to them such as quiet, a desk or breakfast, who they travel with) and their saved details (the name, email and phone a booking is held under). A search needs the city or what they will be near, the nights, how many people and rooms, and the budget. Ask once, in one short message, for whatever is missing.

## Compare

Look wherever rooms are sold: booking sites, the hotels' own pages. No site is required and none is off limits. Compare on the total for the whole stay with taxes and fees, not the nightly price. Note how far each is from where they need to be, what recent guests complain about, whether it can be cancelled free and until when, and whether it is paid now or at the hotel. Check the hotel's own page for the one or two you would offer.

## Offer

Show the best few as cards they can tap, not as lines of text: the name, the area, the price (say whether a night or the whole stay), the rating, and the cancellation terms. Say in one line which you would take and why. In the same message, ask once for any detail the booking needs that you do not hold. Wait for their pick unless they said to just book.

## Before you book

Take the pick to the last step, turning down add-ons they did not ask for. Stop there and show everything it will confirm: the hotel, the room, the dates, the guests, the name it will be held under, the total, what is due now and the cancellation terms. Ask them to confirm those details even when they said to book. Book only after their yes; a charge goes through their device, which shows the merchant and the total. Never type a card number or click the paying button yourself. If the total differs from what you showed, stop and show the new one.

## Afterwards

When it is booked, say in one line: the hotel, the dates, the total, the last day it can be cancelled free, and the address. If nothing could be booked, say so first, then what you tried and the next best thing.
