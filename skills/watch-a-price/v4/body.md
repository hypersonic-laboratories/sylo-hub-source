# Watch a price

Find a product's price now, then keep an eye on it for them. Two parts: the first look, in this conversation, and the watching, which only a routine can do.

## The first look

1. Make sure you know the exact product: the make, the model and the size or colour. If it could mean more than one thing, ask once.
2. Find the target. If they gave a price they would buy at, use it. If not, ask for one. Do not guess.
3. Look across the shops that sell it, wherever they are, and record for each: the shop, the price, whether it is in stock, and the address of the page. A page that will not load as plain text can go to a browser task.
4. Tell them what you found in a few lines: the lowest price now, where, and how far it is from their target.

If it is already at or under the target, say so first, and stop: there is nothing to watch.

## Offer to watch it

If the price is above the target, finish by offering to keep watching: "Want me to make it a routine? I'll check each day and tell you when it drops under the price."

Put in that same message the exact product, the target price and the addresses of the pages you checked, so the routine can be made from the conversation as it stands. Do nothing else for it here: this skill's commands cannot make a routine. When they say yes, the routine is made in the next turn, and the card they approve names this skill, so the routine can follow it.

## When it runs as a routine

If you are reading this inside a routine's run, you do not see the conversation that made it: the routine's own instructions hold the product, the target price and the addresses to check. If any is missing, say so and stop.

In each run, find today's price on those pages. If one is at or under the target, tell them: the shop, the price and the address. If none is, say so in one short line with the lowest price today. Keep a note only when the lowest price changes by more than a few percent, with the product, the price and the date, so the history is there when they ask.

Never buy anything. Say plainly if a page would not load or no longer shows the product.
