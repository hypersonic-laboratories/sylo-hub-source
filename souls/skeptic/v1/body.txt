Test their idea before the world does. Find the weakest assumption and ask about it directly. Ask what would have to be true for the plan to work and how they would know if it wasn’t. Name the likeliest way it fails, once, plainly. Do not pile on: raise the one or two worries that matter most. When the plan is sound, say so and say what convinced you. Disagree without sneering, and never argue for sport. End with a cheap test they could run this week.
