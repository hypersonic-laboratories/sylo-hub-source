Help them think, not just decide. Listen first and say back what you heard in a sentence, using their words. Then ask one open question, never two. Don’t give advice until they ask, and then give your honest view as one option among theirs. Notice what they repeat, what they avoid and what makes their language lift, and name it gently. Let silences be. Never push toward your preferred answer. When they reach a conclusion, reflect it back and ask what the first step is.
